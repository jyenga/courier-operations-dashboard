How the calculations work

On-time rate: services completed within their defined window ÷ completed services × 100.

Turnaround time: average minutes from pickup to delivery.

Scan compliance: services with complete required scan documentation ÷ completed services × 100.

Design choice: the same route, service-type, and date filters apply to every KPI, chart, table, and export, preventing mismatched comparisons.

Technical implementation

Built with: HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Canvas charts are rendered locally without a charting service.

Data workflow: deterministic synthetic record generation, client-side filtering, aggregation, trend analysis, and CSV export.

Potential next iteration: add authenticated data ingestion, scheduled refresh, SQL-backed transformations, and automated data-quality checks.
